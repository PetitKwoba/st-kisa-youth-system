# Provisional Board Policy Decisions

Effective configuration date: 30 June 2026

These decisions resolve the conflicts recorded in the SRD so development can
continue. They are stored as auditable system configuration and should be
ratified in the next Board or AGM resolution.

## Welfare

- Support for the death of an active member is KES 60,000.
- Other funeral, medical, and special-circumstance benefits are configurable
  and require a recorded Board resolution before activation.
- A welfare payment requires Treasurer verification plus approvals from the
  Secretary and Chairperson.
- Available liquidity, membership status, arrears, and supporting evidence must
  be checked before approval.

## Refunds

- The newer refund policy prevails over the earlier constitution wording.
- The processing fee is KES 50.
- The target decision and payment period is 14 days, subject to liquidity.
- Arrears, fines, and other confirmed obligations are deducted before payment.
- No automatic 10% retention applies.
- Where liquidity is insufficient, payment may be scheduled in no more than
  three documented instalments.
- Refunds above KES 2,000 must use M-Pesa or bank transfer.

## Health support

- Payments go directly to a verified hospital, pharmacy, Paybill, or bank
  account. Cash payment to a member is disabled by default.
- An emergency exception requires documented evidence and unanimous approval
  by the Treasurer, Secretary, and Chairperson.
- The contribution window is limited to 10 days.

## Investments

- Allowed instruments are Kenya Treasury bills, Kenya Government bonds, and
  CMA-regulated money market funds.
- A single investment may not exceed 25% of available liquid funds.
- Every investment requires an Executive Committee resolution and approval by
  the Chairperson and Treasurer.
- Maturity, principal, expected income, realized income, institution, and
  approval reference are mandatory.
- The Treasurer reports the investment register monthly.

## Privacy and retention

- Members can view only their own profile, contributions, statements, requests,
  and documents.
- Treasurer access is limited to financial and payment information.
- Secretary access includes membership and governance records.
- Medical and welfare evidence is restricted to the member, Chairperson,
  Secretary, and explicitly assigned welfare reviewers.
- Auditor access is read-only.
- Financial and approval records are retained for seven years.
- Rejected applications and identity documents are retained for two years,
  unless a legal hold applies.
- Downloads and sensitive record changes are audit logged.

## Authentication

- Every user has an individual account; shared official accounts are prohibited.
- Passwords require at least 10 characters and are stored only as strong hashes.
- Sessions use HTTP-only, SameSite cookies and expire after eight hours.
- Officials should use two-factor authentication before production launch.
